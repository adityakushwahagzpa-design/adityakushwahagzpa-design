Text-to-SQL: ask a bank's database in plain English, with a guard that blocked 25 of 25 attacks

Claude writes SQLite for a fictional retail bank (6,000 customers, 144,169 transactions, 1,800 loans), tests it with a run_sql tool, fixes its own errors and explains the answer. A glossary turns NPA, CASA, fiscal year and "active customer" into exact rules, and every query passes a read-only guard.
