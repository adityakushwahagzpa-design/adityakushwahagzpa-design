A LightGBM model forecasts daily revenue for three product lines 90 days ahead, blended with Holt-Winters and given a P10–P90 range. A Claude analyst answers what-if questions by calling the model as a tool, so every number in its answer comes from a model run.

How accurate is it?

Each model forecast Q2, Q3 and Q4 2025, 90 days ahead, from the end of the previous quarter. Daily error (WAPE), lower is better:

The final blend is 95% LightGBM and 5% Holt-Winters; weights were picked by grid search on the backtest. Holt-Winters missed Q4 2025 by ₹14 crore because it cannot see Diwali moving between months.
