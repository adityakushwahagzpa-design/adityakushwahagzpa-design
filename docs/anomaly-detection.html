Real-time anomaly detection: all 7 payment incidents caught, no false alarms in 12 days

A streaming monitor watches every minute of UPI, card and net-banking payments, compares each metric with the same minute on the previous 7 days, and raises a grouped incident with a likely cause. A planned festival sale doubled volume and was correctly ignored.

How it decides

BaselineMedian of the same minute (±15 min) on the previous 7 days, so lunchtime is compared with lunchtime.
ScoreCounts use a Poisson mid-p z-score; ticket size uses a lognormal score. Rates work at 2 a.m. as well as at peak.
RulesA point alert (z ≥ 5), a sustained alert (z ≥ 3.5 for several minutes) and CUSUM for slow drifts.
CalendarVolume is not scored during planned events: 720 volume alerts were suppressed during the festival sale.
GroupingAlerts within 15 minutes become one incident with a lead metric and a likely cause.

Detection speed

The slow failure drift (INC6) took 93 minutes: failures rose gradually, and the CUSUM rule waited until the evidence was clear rather than raising a false alarm. The other six were caught in 0–3 minutes. Five more random seeds: 35 of 35 incidents caught, 1 false alarm in 60 days.
