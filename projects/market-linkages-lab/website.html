Bond: a duration and convexity estimate for a 100bp rise is checked against exact repricing in the workbook; the difference is under ₹0.02 on a ₹100 bond.

The chain

Policy rate by a Taylor rule (r* + inflation + 0.5 × inflation gap + 0.5 × output gap). Curve by Nelson-Siegel, anchored to the policy rate at the short end. Bond priced off the curve with half-yearly coupons.

Currencies and equities

USD/INR forward by covered interest parity. Cost of equity by CAPM on the 10-year yield; value by the Gordon growth model, D1 / (ke − g). Simplified: no credit spread, flat dividend path.
