Initial measurement. Lease liability = present value of the fixed lease payments, discounted at the incremental borrowing rate because the rate implicit in the lease is not readily determinable. Right-of-use asset = liability + initial direct costs.

Subsequent measurement. Each month the liability grows by interest (opening balance × rate / 12) and falls by the rent paid. The asset is depreciated straight line over the lease term, as ownership does not transfer.

Profit and loss. Interest is highest at the start, so the total charge exceeds the cash rent in early years and falls below it later. Operating cash flow improves, because the principal part of each payment is a financing cash flow.

Balance sheet. The current portion of the liability is the principal that falls due within twelve months of the reporting date; the rest is non-current. The maturity analysis shows undiscounted payments, as Ind AS 107 requires.

Simplifications. No extension or termination option, no residual value guarantee, no variable payments, no lease incentive and no remeasurement events. Short-term and low-value exemptions do not apply to this lease.
